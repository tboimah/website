One platform, built to strengthen that connection on both sides.

Monthly · Live Event Series · In Collaboration with Eaton House DC

Creators
Jam Live.

In an age where music feels limitless and free, the artists making it are still bound by an industry built to extract value from their work without compensating them for it. Creators Jam Live exists to push back on that — and for the audience, it's a front-row seat: come discover DMV artists before anyone else knows their name, and watch a song get built live instead of just hearing the finished version.

Creators Jam Live began in 2021 on the rooftop of the Eaton Hotel — an open, informal space for DC artists to create together in front of a live audience. After a hiatus, Michael returned to Eaton as an artist-in-residence, and in collaboration with Program Director Pete Danielski and visual artist Khadija Jahmila, staged Free Music: The Illusion of Liberation — an exhibition naming the unseen labor and financial constraints most listeners never see, and framing the whole thing not just as critique, but as a reclamation of artistic autonomy. The companion recording was later named an Honorable Mention on CapitalBop's list of the best D.C. jazz albums of 2025.

From there, Creators Jam Live came back with a new format and a sharper purpose. This isn't a showcase where artists perform for other artists — it's built for artist development. The jam session, The Write Off songwriting contest, and the live visual art all exist to help artists sharpen their craft in front of a real audience, not just their peers, and build the kind of fan base that sticks — people who connect with the artist specifically, not just the moment. That's what turns a room into a following, and a following into a career.

About Capital Music Network

Built From
Within.

Capital Music Network is a program of Distrik Kollective Entertainment Inc. (DKE) — a Washington, DC 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2021 by bassist Michael Bowie. It started the way most of DKE's work starts: from the inside, from a musician who'd lived the problem CMN now exists to solve — artists doing the work and not owning the outcome.

Since then, DKE has grown from a single rooftop jam session into a full ecosystem: Creators Jam Live, a monthly artist development series; CMN On Demand, a creator-owned streaming platform; Inside the Craft, an original conversation series; and Capital Music Network itself, tying it all together. A fiscal sponsorship program — giving affiliated artists the infrastructure to receive grant funding for their own projects — is in development and will be offered in a future phase.

600+ DMV artists engaged. Funded twice by the DC Commission on the Arts and Humanities. Partners and collaborators include Howard University's Chadwick A. Boseman College of Fine Arts, Eaton House DC, Atlas Performing Arts Center, and the Mid Atlantic Jazz Festival. Current event sponsors include Chuck Levin's Washington Music Center and Rocklands BBQ — with more in development.
